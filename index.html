How To Join

LuminoServer runs Geyser & Floodgate, so both Java and Bedrock players can play together on the same world.

Java Edition

  1. Open Minecraft: Java Edition and click Multiplayer
  2. Click Add Server
  3. Paste the address below as the Server Address
  4. Click Done, then join the server!
play.luminoserver.example.com

Bedrock Edition

  1. Open Minecraft (Bedrock) and go to Play → Servers
  2. Scroll down and click Add Server
  3. Enter the IP and Port shown below
  4. Save and join the server!
bedrock.luminoserver.example.com : 19132

Server Info

Multiple worlds, multiple ways to play — pick your adventure.

Survival / Overworld

The main world. Build, mine, farm, and team up with other players in classic survival mode with land claims enabled.

PvP Arena

Gear up and battle it out in dedicated arenas. Loot drops, kill streaks, and bragging rights — no grief risk to your base.

Resource World

A separate, regularly-reset world for mining and resource gathering, so your main survival world stays untouched.

The End

Challenge the Ender Dragon, raid End Cities, and collect Elytra and shulker shells with your team.

The Nether

Fast-travel hub and loot-rich danger zone. Build portals, farm blaze rods, and watch out for piglins.

Rules

Keep it fun for everyone. Breaking these may result in a mute, kick, or ban.

  • 01 No griefing or stealing from other players' builds or claims.
  • 02 No cheats, hacked clients, x-ray, or exploiting bugs.
  • 03 No spamming chat, ads, or excessive caps.
  • 04 Be respectful — no harassment, hate speech, or slurs.
  • 05 PvP is only allowed in the PvP Arena unless both players agree.
  • 06 No inappropriate builds, skins, or usernames.
  • 07 Do not impersonate staff or share others' personal info.
  • 08 Follow staff instructions — decisions can be appealed on Discord.
